OUR PHILOSOPHY

Crafted For Those Who Value Time.

VELORA was created with one simple idea — a watch should be more than an instrument that tells time.

It should reflect personality, craftsmanship and the moments that define us.

From the first sketch to the final polished case, every VELORA timepiece is designed with attention to detail. We combine contemporary aesthetics with traditional watchmaking principles to create pieces that remain timeless.
